Music discovery with Discogs and MCP.

Research mixes with your AI client, find the original releases and save tracks to crates. Browse Discogs and preview the records in a desktop app.

Mix research with MCP

Use a compatible MCP client such as Claude or Codex to research a mix, search Discogs and add the releases to a crate. Mark the matching tracks and keep source links, tags and unresolved candidates with the records.

Enable AI mix research in Settings. Install the Claude extension or connect Codex. The app must stay open. Your client handles the research; Auto Digger provides the Discogs and crate tools.

Set up Claude or Codex

In the app

Discogs discovery
Filter by label, artist, year, genre, style and release type. Browse artwork or choose a random match.
Audio
Preview available YouTube sources before downloading. Save tracks with Discogs tags and artwork, then play them locally.
Crates
Save releases in named crates with track picks and personal tags. Search downloaded tracks and open their folders.
Appearance
Six materials, seven accents and light or dark mode. Choose a download folder and flat or per-release storage.
