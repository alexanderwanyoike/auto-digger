Auto Digger

A quiet desk for discovering records.

Follow a label, explore a style or dig up a random release on Discogs. Download audio to listen locally and keep your finds in named crates.

DownloadsRun from source

In the app

Discogs discovery
Filter by label, artist, year or range, genre, style and release type. Save filters for later or choose a random match.
Audio
Download available YouTube sources with Discogs tags and artwork, then play them locally.
Soulseek
Choose Soulseek in Audio sources to find audio through your slskd instance. Refine the search, compare public matches and save a file into your Auto Digger library. Set up Soulseek.
Crates
Save releases in named crates with track picks and personal tags. Search downloaded tracks and open their folders.
Appearance
Six materials, seven accents and light or dark mode. Organise new downloads with source, genre, year, label, artist and release folder placeholders.

Mix research with MCP

Connect an MCP client such as Claude or Codex for mix research. Your client can research a mix, search Discogs and add the releases to a crate, with matching track picks, source links, tags and unresolved candidates.

Enable Auto Digger MCP in Settings. Install the Claude extension or connect Codex. The app must stay open. Your client handles the research; Auto Digger provides the Discogs and crate tools.
