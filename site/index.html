MCP is the way in

Research has
receipts.

Your AI client researches the mix and checks candidate recordings. Auto Digger looks up Discogs metadata, saves the verified releases into a crate and keeps the matched tracks, personal tags, source links and unresolved leads together.

CONNECT

Your client. Your session.

Enable AI mix research in Settings and copy the MCP configuration into a compatible client, such as Claude or Codex. Keep the app open and connect your Discogs account.

INVESTIGATE

Follow the actual releases.

Ask the host to research a mix or compilation, verify track versions and prefer original singles or EPs. It can search Discogs and create a named crate as it works.

COME BACK

Keep the trail.

Review marked tracks, tags and source notes beside the saved records. Uncertainty stays visible. Access can be switched off and starts disabled each time the app opens.

MCP setup and available tools ↗

Experimental workflow. Your AI host needs its own web research capabilities. Auto Digger verifies catalogue metadata; matching a recording and resolving uncertainty still needs judgement.

Then put your ears to work

Keep digging.
Make it your own.

Take over at the desk. Follow a label, explore a year or combine genres, styles and release types. Draw a random match, preview the available audio and keep what stands out.

Follow a thread.

Searchable multi-select filters, sleeve artwork and random releases. Start narrow or leave room for a surprise.

02 / LISTEN

Preview before download.

Play available YouTube sources in the bottom player. Keep individual tracks or review a whole release, with progress and best-effort Discogs tags.

03 / KEEP

Find it again.

Named crates, track picks, personal tags and a searchable audio library. Local files, with a folder button when you need them.
