A direction, not a destination

Start with a sound.
See where it goes.

A favourite label. A particular year. That style you keep coming back to. Set the boundaries, then dig into random matching releases.

01

Find a thread.

Combine artists, labels, years, genres, styles and release types. Pick several, or keep things open. Artwork helps you recognise the records along the way.

02

Listen before you keep it.

Preview an available YouTube source in the player. Download the tracks you want, or review a whole release. Discogs supplies tags and cover art where available.

03

Make a crate of it.

Save the release, pick the tracks that stand out and add your own tags. Come back to a named crate when it is time to listen again.

The ones worth coming back to

A place for
your finds.

Crates are your playlists of releases. Keep a late-night selection, a label rabbit hole or records for the next set. Your picks, tags and source notes stay with them.

Listen on your desktop.

A searchable Downloads list, one player and a folder button when you need the files.

Keep your place.

Your active crate returns when you reopen the app. Saved records can be browsed offline.

Follow it further.

Jump to Discogs sellers, or search Beatport, Traxsource and Bandcamp for the record.

An experimental way in

Bring a mix back
to its records.

Connect a compatible AI client through local MCP tools. Ask it to research a mix, verify the releases and save its findings into a crate, with track picks and sources for you to check.
